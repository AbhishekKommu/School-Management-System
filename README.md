## 💡 How It Works

1. Admin logs into the system.
2. Students, teachers, classes, and subjects are managed through the dashboard.
3. Teachers can record attendance and manage academic information.
4. Exams and student results can be recorded and viewed.
5. Fees and payment records can be maintained.
6. Administrators can generate reports and monitor school activities.
